School jokes: clean and funny

Ten clean jokes about homework, exams, teachers and report cards. Safe to share in a class group, and gentle enough that teachers laugh too.

  • Teacher: "Aman, why are your exam answers written so small?"
    Aman: "Small mistakes get smaller red marks, ma'am."

  • Our maths teacher said, "Show your working."
    So I drew a picture of myself sweating.

  • Teacher: "Your handwriting is like a doctor's."
    Student: "Thank you, sir. Now only the marks are left."

  • I told my mother I got 100 in the exams.
    Then she asked for the subject-wise list.
    It was 30, 25, 25 and 20.

  • The school bell is the only speaker whose shortest speech gets the loudest cheer.

  • Online class taught me one thing.
    The mute button works on students, but never on the neighbour's pressure cooker.

  • Teacher: "If you have ten laddoos and I take away four, what do you have?"
    Student: "A complaint for my mother."

  • My geometry box has a compass, a divider and a protractor.
    I have used them exactly once.
    To open a packet of chips.

  • Report card day is the only day I walk home slowly and still reach too early.

  • Teacher: "Why is your bag so light today?"
    Student: "The homework is at home, sir. It needed rest."
