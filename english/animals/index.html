Animal jokes for kids

Ten silly, clean animal jokes for children. They are short enough to remember and tell again at school the next day.

  • Why did the tortoise order food online?
    He wanted something to reach home before he did.

  • What do crows hold on the terrace every morning?
    A caw-nference.

  • Why did the snail buy a scooter?
    It was tired of hearing "Hurry up!"

  • Why was the parrot made class monitor?
    It could repeat every rule perfectly.

  • Why does the rooster never buy an alarm clock?
    He is the alarm, and he has no snooze button.

  • Why did the peacock check the weather app?
    To plan its dance practice.

  • What did the buffalo say to the pond in May?
    "You are my only air conditioner."

  • Why did the mosquito get a job at the radio station?
    It was already good at buzzing in people's ears.

  • Why did the camel carry no water bottle on the school trip?
    It said, "I filled up last week."

  • Why did the cat sit on the laptop?
    To keep an eye on the mouse.
