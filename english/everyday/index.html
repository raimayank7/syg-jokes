Everyday life jokes: travel, weather and neighbours

Ten clean jokes about autos, trains, power cuts, rain and the neighbourhood. Small troubles of daily life, seen from the funny side.

  • The auto driver's meter is out of order every day.
    His arithmetic never is.

  • "I am just five minutes away" is not a time. It is a feeling.

  • On the day you dry clothes on the terrace, the rain remembers your address.

  • During a power cut, the whole colony meets on the balconies.
    That is the real society meeting.

  • Map app: "You have arrived at your destination."
    Me, standing in the middle of a field: "Which destination?"

  • The upper berth of a train:
    climbing up is exercise, climbing down is courage.

  • I chose a gym close to my house so that I would have no excuse.
    Now I take the long road home.

  • Taking a bath in winter is brave.
    Taking one every day deserves a medal.

  • The bus said "Limited stops".
    The stops turned out to be unlimited.

  • In a lift, everybody stands silent and watches the floor numbers,
    as if it were a cricket score.
